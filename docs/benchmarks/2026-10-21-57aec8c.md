# FastAPI-RS single-workload benchmark at `57aec8c`

## Result

The `large-response-model-asgi` workload passed its identity-checked parity
gate (**1/1 selected observation**) before timing. On this single host,
FastAPI-RS had 1.123× the FastAPI median latency and 1.224× the p95 latency.
This refresh covers one of the seven declared direct-ASGI workloads; it does
not refresh the full suite or establish a general performance conclusion.

| Subject | Median latency | p95 latency | Request-loop throughput |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FastAPI 0.141.1 | 361.8 µs | 387.9 µs | 2,554 requests/s |
| FastAPI-RS | 406.2 µs | 474.7 µs | 2,225 requests/s |

Ratios are FastAPI-RS divided by FastAPI. Request-loop throughput also includes
per-call setup, response observation, correctness checks, and loop bookkeeping;
it is separate from per-request latency.

## Measurement boundary

Per-request latency times only `await app(scope, receive, send)`. It includes
FastAPI response-model validation and JSON serialization, the FastAPI-RS
facade, PyO3 conversion and native execution, ASGI dispatch, response creation,
and captured sends. It excludes interpreter startup, app and route setup,
payload generation during construction, callback construction, response
observation and correctness checks, loop bookkeeping, HTTP server work, and
network or client overhead. Both subjects ran sequentially at concurrency 1
with 50 warmups and five rounds of 200 samples; all 1,000 latency samples per
subject were retained.
